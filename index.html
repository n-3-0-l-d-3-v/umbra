Method

How a flight works
A

Take the seat

On Manual, the simulation stops at every point where the algorithm has to choose. Nothing moves until you decide.

B

Make the call

Pick right and the run continues. Pick wrong and you are told exactly why the algorithm would have chosen differently.

C

Get debriefed

Your moves are compared with par, the fewest decisions the algorithm needs. Autopilot replays any round with a full explanation.
